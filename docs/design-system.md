# Semli design system

Decided October 19, 2026. The live, readable version is the design-system page: https://claude.ai/artifact/Y3JnVE1gxVuvGrxjq795Z2. The review canvas with every screen is https://claude.ai/artifact/QgE4ddmEytZ2PyHMCT3QoV. Keep this file and the page in step.

## Direction
Robinhood's 2024 identity is the reference: neutrals first, one accent used sparingly, a simplified mark next to a wordmark, one typeface, "less is more". Smoothness is discipline, not the language the app is written in.

- Ink on paper. About 72% of any screen is paper and surfaces, 18% ink, 7% grey rows, 3% accent.
- One big number per screen (Regular, 56px). Everything under it is one step smaller than iOS defaults. Contrast reads as premium, size does not.
- Color only when it means something: terracotta is behind or in conflict, green is ahead, amber is "Semli needs something from you".
- Pills act, rectangles hold. Every action is fully round; rows, cards, fields and composers keep corners. Corners grow with the thing, from `radius` in `packages/tokens` (Oct 23, 2026): `mark` 6 for chart blocks and tiny squares (a checkbox, a keycap), `small` 10 for anything under about 44pt tall (press and hover highlights, hour-view blocks, compact fields), `field` 16 for content (fields, notes, the composer, cards, thumbnails), `container` 24 for dialogs and panels, `pill` for every action. Something inset in a rounded container takes its radius minus the inset, so the curves run parallel. Rectangles use `corner()` from the theme, which draws Apple's continuous curve on iOS; no hand-picked radii.
- Planner screens use plain rows, with no decorative cards or icon tiles. Photography belongs on moment days, showcase pages and Today's sky (a time-of-day sky fading into the paper behind Today's header only; decided Oct 21, 2026, spec in `headers.md`); the Plus welcome and guide can use small accent icon badges.
- Marketing surfaces (the site, the paywall, the App Store screenshots, the header and search card, the emails) may be more playful than planner screens: a site photo under a scrim, soft lavender paper, one accent word per headline, always bold and large enough to read over the background. The in-app rules above still hold on Today, Plan, School and every row. Decided Oct 20, 2026.
- The website in light mode uses white paper (#FFFFFF) and neutral grey panels (#F4F4F5) instead of the app's cream. Its hero uses a sky photo under an ink veil, with white copy and the shared pale purple accent. Site-only paper overrides live in `apps/web/app/tokens.ts`.

## Brand
- Mark: two dots (big = today, small accent = next), ratio 240 : 102 on a fixed diagonal. `design/icon-b-dots.svg`.
- Wordmark: lowercase `semli.` in Onest SemiBold; the tittle of the i and the full stop are the two dots. Outlines are generated with `design/generate-wordmark.py`. Mark and wordmark use the app's ink and accent values, never their own.
- Where it appears: app icon, splash, onboarding welcome, account and sharing previews, settings footer, website, store. Never on Today, Plan or School. "You" is the fourth tab; account entry lives in its settings rows. Your plan is the single subscription entry; renewal details, Restore and Manage subscription live inside that page.
- Launch splash: white (#FFFFFF) with the ink Onest wordmark and its original purple dots in both light and dark mode, centered at 240 points wide. Render the transparent PNG at the wordmark's natural aspect ratio, with no square padding. Use contain scaling; no photo, tagline, or loading delay. Native splash changes require a new build and release-build verification.

Light mode keeps the original paper-and-ink treatment: primary actions, send, selected tabs/dates, chips and segmented controls use ink. Booked graph marks use muted plum (`#76658F`) in light mode, with white text on filled hourly events. Purple stays in the original small accents such as links, Plus, today labels and brand dots. Dark mode extends lavender to those interactive controls, disclosure chevrons, and booked events across day, hourly, week and term graphs. Use `controlFill/controlInk`, `selectionFill/selectionInk`, `disclosureInk`, and `chartBooked/chartOnBooked` so both appearances stay consistent. Body text stays ink; green means done, terracotta means a conflict, and amber means input is needed. Course and category tints are a 10px dot beside text, never a fill or a bar. A course's tint is the person's to choose: the course page has a Color row of the six tints as round swatches, the chosen one ringed in ink like a picked mark (Oct 23, 2026). New courses take the least-used tint, so up to six never share one and a removed course's colour is reused first. Lavender is the brand accent, so a new course takes it only after the other five (sky first on a tie, Oct 23, 2026); a course that already has lavender keeps it. Six only for now: a seventh tint name would crash older builds that sync the course before they know it. In `ItemRow` and the web board a class's to-dos draw their check ring in the course tint (2pt while open, green once done), as Apple Reminders does per list, and its events lead with the dot, so a week reads by course without a tinted card. The dot is 10px (Oct 23, 2026): hue is hard to tell apart on marks this small, and 6 then 8 were too small to tell six tints apart. The 3pt gutter bar tried on Oct 21, 2026 was removed Oct 23 for reading as template UI.

Meaning: filled terracotta pill for due today / overdue; terracotta text on a grey pill for a colliding time; green pill for done and newly added, green check when complete; grey pills for times and neutral states; amber only when Semli needs input. Filled status colors use `onPrimary` for legible text and icons in either theme.

## Type
Geist carries product text: normal / Regular 400 for all large headings, the one big number, section titles and text; Medium 500 for pills and the primary label; and SemiBold 600 for emphasis. All display tokens share `largeHeadingWeight` in the tokens package; large headings use these tokens rather than local font-family or bold overrides. Onboarding follows this same normal-weight rule. The separate outlined wordmark uses Onest SemiBold. Tabular numerals wherever a number can change. Sentence case always.

EB Garamond Regular is the marketing voice: the serif was decided Oct 20, 2026 (Newsreader), and switched to EB Garamond Regular on Oct 21, 2026. It appears only through `displaySerif` (34 / 40) on lines that speak to the student: the paywall tagline, the Plus welcome and the auth welcome. On the site it is the `.serif` class on the hero, pricing and closing headlines. On Oct 20, 2026 the App Store captions were Geist Bold; on Oct 21, 2026 the founder moved them to the serif, the site's h1 face (Regular, tracked 1.5% tight, centered, ink with one lavender accent word), all ten on one background, the morning sky from Today's header (`design/render-screenshots.py`). The italic is for store art (screenshots, header, search card) and the site's serif accent word (the hero's "organized", pricing's "Plus"); the app stays upright. It never carries a number, a row, a form heading, a question step or a section title; if every heading were serif, none would be special.

## Errors

Use a short, action-specific sentence and a useful next step: “We couldn’t send a test reminder. Please try again.” Technical details belong in diagnostics, not toasts, fields or alerts. Auth, purchases, imports, Canvas, reminders, alarms, export and backup use named error boundaries; known cancellations remain quiet. Unexpected screen failures have a plain retry screen, and old links offer Open Semli.

`reportError` records a specific operation, error name, code, status and sanitized message/stack once per exception. `errorDiagnostic` allowlists fields and redacts credentials, codes, URLs, email addresses and local usernames; request bodies, session objects, receipts, calendar feeds and uploaded files are never attached. Configured Sentry receives the sanitized exception. Import responses must use known shared product copy before the client displays them.

A failed local save keeps the current plan in memory and shows a persistent save warning with Retry. A failed read/decode shows recovery before the planner opens and blocks replacement writes. Recovery never clears a saved plan. Test-reminder success appears only after native scheduling succeeds; a failed confirmation resend still allows entering the latest emailed code and explains that a fresh code was not sent.

## Buttons
Five roles, all fully round, press shrinks 3% and dims to 88% on `spring.snappy`, one haptic per role.
1. Primary: ink in light mode, purple in dark, with `controlInk` text, 52 high, one per screen. Haptic tap.
2. Secondary: grey fill, same pill. Chips are the 40-high version; tiles (icon + label) the 64-high version. One component shared by capture and onboarding.
3. Text: accent, underlined (offset 5, 1.5px); muted variant in the same accent without underline. Haptic select.
4. Icon: 44 circle, grey or ink. The glass plus in the tab bar is the one 56 exception.
5. Destructive: a text button in terracotta, confirms inline. No filled capsule, no SwiftUI bordered button.

Rows are not buttons: they highlight on press and never shrink. Icons in ink, no tiles behind them. The onboarding choice card (two lines) is the one rectangular secondary, at the `field` radius.

## Chrome and inputs
- App copy states the fact or action in plain language, including onboarding, sign-in, Plus and notifications. Avoid slogans, motivational filler and vague phrases such as “More life. Less juggling.” or “nowhere else.” Name the item, date, time or next action. Say “Saved on this iPhone” for local storage and “Daily summary” for the schedule notification. Only promise reminders or automatic updates when permissions and plan access support them.
- Copy uses sentence case for headings, controls, status labels, and standalone date labels: "Next due", "Today", "In 3 days", "Active". Keep units and words within a sentence lowercase ("2 courses", "4:00 pm", "Due today"); preserve names and imported titles.
- Expandable rows use one chevron, pointing right when closed and down when expanded. Rotate it smoothly over the shared base duration; preserve the expanded accessibility state and honor Reduce Motion.
- Main tabs use `TabHeader` (decided Oct 21, 2026; `headers.md`): the same 24 horizontal margin and safe-area top + 20, at most one small line above the big value, and the meaning under it. Today, Plan's day and School show the date on that line; You and Add show none. Numbers and countdowns are `displayXl` 56/60 ("34m", "3 days", "Today"); word titles are `display` 34/40 ("October", "You", "Add", "School" before a course exists). The line under the value finishes it in words only ("until Stats lecture ends"); the time leads the grey line under it ("10:15 am · STAT 200 · Coursework"), so a long title wraps on its own and never strands a time (`countdownParts` in `@dayli/core`, Oct 21, 2026). Details sit 12 below the value, with 4 between related lines. Today's sky (`SkyHeader`) sits behind Today's header; moment photos replace it.
- Plan's title is the selected month ("October", with the year only outside the current year), which opens a Monday-first month grid below it with month browsing and a Today shortcut. No date line and no week arrows: the chart swipes by week, VoiceOver has Next and Previous week actions on each day, and a round Today pill appears top right only when another week is showing. The week's totals sit under the chart with the chart key under them; "Swipe for next week" shows until the person first moves by week (`firsts.week`). A date selection updates the chart, agenda and saved Plan date together and closes the grid. The selected day's column carries the same ink focus ring as a picked mark (`focusGap` 2, 1.5 stroke), springing in with the date pill, so the chosen day reads in the graph itself and follows it when the week moves.
- Headers: system bar, native SF Symbol chevron and X in glass on iOS 26 (our 1.75-stroke icons in grey circles elsewhere), Geist 16 Medium title, paper background, no shadow, hairline only when content scrolls under.
- Fields: one line is a pill (52 high); rest grey with no border, focus white with a 1.5px ink ring. Never a blue ring.
- Composer: white rectangle at the `field` radius, attachment icons inside, send as an ink circle. Replaces textarea plus tiles.
- Date and time fields: grey pills (36 high) that open the native picker under their row in one tap, the way Calendar does: a wheel for a time, the month for a date. The open pill turns white with an ink ring; tapping it again closes the picker. Never a pill that swaps in a compact picker, which needs a second tap for its popover.
- Toggle: green when on in both themes, grey when off, white thumb. All switches share this default, including settings, create, and the paywall. Segmented control: sliding pill with a selection tick. Check: grey ring, green when done. Radio: ink dot.
- Progress: six-segment ink bar in onboarding; a 2px line under the header while loading. Toast: a pill that hugs its sentence, centred above the tab bar, in inverted ink (ink on a light page, near-white `inkPrimary` on a dark one, so it never sinks into the dark canvas), message in callout, Undo in subhead, a soft shadow in light mode only; it rises on the bounded timed curve. Toasts are for changes made without a confirming screen: delete, add, move, spread, import, and completions made by swipe or Mark all done ("Done: Essay." with Undo). Tapping a checkbox gets none; tapping it again is the undo.
- Controls use Semli's palette: no default blue tint; toggle green comes from `added`.

## Navigation
- Liquid Glass tab bar with five tabs: Today, Plan, School, You, and Add. Add carries the `search` role, so iOS 26 draws it as the detached circle beside the main pill (the pattern helthy uses for its fifth tab); older iOS shows it as a trailing tab. Every "add" entry point navigates to the Add tab, with `?source=image|file` to open a picker on arrival. There is no add menu.
- Chrome buttons at the top of a screen (the Today list/graph toggle, close, back, the onboarding chevron) sit in a Liquid Glass circle on iOS 26 (`components/Glass.tsx`: `GlassCircle`, `GlassGroup` for several together), and in the grey circle elsewhere.
- Detail screens push with one glass back chevron on the left (item detail, every settings page). Set this explicitly even for the first route in a nested stack: opening Appearance from the You tab does not give it a native back button automatically.
- Create, Review, Account preview and Share schedule preview slide up as full-screen covers with one glass X on the left. Add is a tab; `/capture` redirects there. No half sheets for primary flows.
- `navigationButtonOptions` in `components/NavButtons.tsx` owns header controls: one native glass item on iOS 26, one circular fallback elsewhere, automatic back hidden to prevent duplicate controls. Configure it in the navigator before mounting; `Sheet` only updates it for a custom close action. Back returns through the parent stack when needed, or goes home when opened without history.

## Tokens, not numbers (Oct 23, 2026)
Every repeated value has a name in `packages/tokens`, re-exported by `apps/mobile/src/theme`; a hand-typed number is a bug unless it is one of a kind.
- Type: `lead` (18/26) is the sentence under a screen's big number, `titleSm` (20/26) a sheet's or dialog's title, `axis` (11/14) chart axis, day and key labels only. Pushed screens take `headerTitle()` for the system header.
- Spacing: `screen.settingsBody` for every settings page (16 top, groups 24 apart, 48 to scroll past the end) and `screen.section` for a section on a tab or detail page (24 above). Every padding, margin and gap comes from `space` (hair 2, xxs 4, snug 6, xs 8, sm 12, md 16, lg 20, xl 24, xxl 32, xxxl 40); on Oct 23, 2026 the off-scale values were snapped (10 and 14 to 12, 18 to 16, 22–28 to 24, 30 and 34 to 32). Charts keep their own geometry from `chart`.
- Sizes: `size.control` 52 for buttons and pill fields, `size.pill` 36 for in-row actions, `size.chip` 28 for status pills, `size.target` 44 the least tap height; rows are `size.compact` 48 (choice and scope rows), `size.row` 56 (settings and plan rows) or `size.item` 64 (agenda items). Icons from `iconSize`: hero 24, nav 22, row 20, title chevron 18, disclosure chevron 16, inline 14. Every chevron is `disclosureInk`.
- State: `fade.pressed` 0.6 for a pressed row (rows dim, never shrink), `fade.disabled` 0.4; `Press` shrinks by `motion.press.scale`, or `iconScale` (0.9) for a small round icon button.
- Floating: `c.backdrop` behind dialogs (darker in dark mode), `shadow.dialog`, `shadow.float` and `shadow.pane`. `c.scrim` stays the site's photo scrim.
- Shared pieces: `Radio`, `Button`, `CategoryDot` and `Check` in components; `count`, `andList`, `clockTime`/`clockPeriod`, `longMonthDay`, `monthName`, `hoursMinutes` and `spanMinutes` in `@dayli/core`; `sourceName` in `lib/source-name.ts`.

## Motion
Tokens in `packages/tokens/src/motion.ts`, presets in `apps/mobile/src/lib/motion.ts`, rules in `docs/motion.md`. Two springs (snappy 37/380/0.9, gentle 28/190/1), both with at least critical damping and overshoot clamped; three durations (120/220/360), one easing (0.2, 0, 0, 1), press 0.97/0.88, stagger 45ms. Layout and entrances use the bounded timed curve; completion rings stay fixed as their fill and tick grow in. Numbers roll; every wait has a skeleton; one haptic per meaning. Motion settles once, without bounce-back.

## Charts
One chart, the timeline, at three zooms. `DayBar` is the day as a horizontal bar (with `highlight` for the item screen). `HourView` is the same day opened by the hour, behind the top-right toggle on Today. `WeekChart` is seven vertical day bars, the Plan header and the schedule review. `TermLine` is a span with dots and rings, the four-week map on School and the syllabus review. Marks everywhere: booked work plum (`chartBooked`, #76658F light / lavender #C3AFF8 dark), booked everything else slate (`chartOther`, #2F6E8F / #8FC0E0), green done or new, grey past, terracotta overlap or dropped, dashed outline where a shift was, dot due, ring quiz or test, ring with a centre (target) exam, hairline now, amber outline needs a time. Shape is the kind of mark, colour is its status, and each colour has one meaning on a chart (Oct 23, 2026): terracotta is only an overlap or a dropped shift, never a due date, so something due today is an ink mark beside the now line; green is only what was marked done or is new, never just past, so an event that ended without a tick is grey and never ticked in the hour view. Due marks (dot, ring, target) are ink on every chart, the week chart, the term line and their keys, never plum or slate, so a deadline never reads as work or other booked time and still reads on a block of either hue through its halo (Oct 23, 2026). Only to-dos are ticked off; events never are, so Plan's week chart and the day bar, which draw only events, have no green block and no "Done" in their key. Booked blocks take one of two hues, work or not (Oct 23, 2026, founder's call after a lighter plum and a narrower block were tried): `bookedColors` picks plum for a shift (work category or an employer) and slate for classes, study and personal plans, in the week chart, day bar and hour view; status colours still win over both. Slate sits at the plum's contrast on the track (4.9:1 light, 7.5:1 dark) and far from terracotta, green and amber. Only those two: the apricot, mint and lavender category tints sit too close to the status colours, so a course's or category's colour stays the 10px dot beside its name in rows. Plan's key names them "Work" and "Other", and VoiceOver counts them apart ("2 shifts, 1 other event"). The schedule review and employer charts hold only shifts and stay plum.

The day bar and Plan's week chart cover their own hours, not 6 am to midnight (Oct 23, 2026): `chartRange` in `packages/core` runs from half an hour before the first thing to half an hour after the last, on whole hours, with now included where the now line shows, and at least six hours for a day, eight for a week. Nothing timed falls back to 6 am to midnight. The day bar's end labels name its real ends, flush with them, with every third hour between (every sixth on a long day) only where it keeps `labelGap` from its neighbours by measured width; the week chart names its range beside the first bar, flush with the bar's top and bottom. While a finger scrubs Plan's week, the line under the chart reads out that day ("Wed 28 · 1 class · 1 shift · 4 work hours") and rolls back to the week when it lets go.

Every line and dot on a chart is placed, not drawn by eye (decided Oct 21, 2026):
- One geometry. Sizes come from `chart` in `packages/tokens`: track 6, dot 8, ring 12 with a 2 stroke, now hairline 1.5 overhanging the track by 5, hour-view now dot 7. The site's mockups (`apps/web/components/mock.module.css`) use the same numbers. No mark picks its own size.
- Centred on the value. Dots, rings and the now line go through `Mark` and `NowLine` (`components/ChartMark.tsx`), which hang each mark from a zero-size anchor at its value and centre it by half its own size. Never position a mark with a hand-tuned `top` or `marginLeft`.
- Nothing hangs off the end. A line with marks insets its plot by half a ring, and its scale and labels share that inset, so a mark on the first or last day sits inside the rounded cap. Column marks clamp inside the column.
- The now hairline carries a canvas halo (`halo` each side) inside the track, like every mark, so it reads where it crosses a block in either mode (ink on lavender was 1.8:1 in dark mode without it); the overhang past the track stays bare, and the day bar draws the halo only where now falls on a block, since Today's bar sits on a photo. An overlap that has ended goes grey with its blocks on the day bar and in the hour view, as on Plan; on an item's own page overlaps stay red. The hour view's now line runs under its blocks and needs none.
- One stacking order: track, blocks, overlap spans, now hairline, then dots and rings, with rings over dots and red over ink. Rings knock out the track under them (surface fill), so a ring never reads as a dot.
- Collisions resolve, never pile up. When two labels under a line would touch, the later one is dropped and its mark stays; labels keep at least `labelGap` (8) between them. Two marks on one date show the more important one on top, and every dot and ring carries a 1.5 canvas halo, so a mark on a block of its own colour or next to another mark still reads as its own.
- Text never sits on a mark or a line. Axis labels stand on their value (Noon centred on noon, the ends flush with the bar's ends), never spread by `space-between`. A month with too few days on the line to hold its name keeps its space and drops its name. In the hour view, the current time takes the gutter and the hour label it would cover steps aside; the now line runs under event blocks, never through their titles.
- One answer for "is this a test". `assessment(title)` in `packages/core` decides exam (midterm, final, exam), quiz (quiz, test) or neither, skipping look-alikes such as a final project, an exam review or a practice quiz. The import's `exam` icon, every chart's shape (`shapeFor`) and the term's end all use it. No screen keeps its own pattern.
- A mark answers "what is this?" in place. Where a line is interactive (School's four-week map), a tap anywhere on a course's line picks the nearest mark within 22 points, rings it in ink (`selected`, `focusGap` 2) and shows that item as a real `ItemRow` under the map; tapping it again clears it. The count beside the title ("4 due") is a text button that lists everything on the map, which is also the VoiceOver path. No tooltips or popovers over the chart.
- Every chart explains its marks. A chart with anything beyond plain bars has a `ChartKey` under it: each mark drawn at its real size beside one word ("Work", "Other", "Due", "Quiz", "Exam", "Done", "Past", "Overlap", "Needs a time"), only for marks on screen now, on a single row (labels shrink slightly on a narrow screen, never wrap). The key stays; it is not a first-run hint. Plan, School, the course page and Review carry one.
- Straight, flat and quiet: no hand-drawn SVG paths, curves, glows, shadows, gradients or decorative gridlines. Hour rules are hairlines in `line`; axis labels are caption at 11 in the secondary ink, sentence case.

## Deleting and editing
One tap deletes, the toast carries Undo, and Your data keeps it undoable after. Rows swipe right to complete and left to delete (`SwipeRow`). A repeating item asks the scope inline under Delete: Just this one, This week, From now on. Review never blocks: untimed shifts go in with an amber "Add a time" pill, undated items are skipped by default ("Add 14 · skip 2"), a class with no end date asks "Until when?" with a 15-week default.

Countdown to the next commitment as the big number, the day as a timeline bar (green done, grey past, plum work and slate everything else, terracotta overlaps, hairline now), then "On your list" and "Coming up" at 24 Regular with chevrons, rows with status pills. "On your list" leads with untimed to-dos, as Plan does, then the day in time order, done last; the "Tomorrow" peek alone stays in time order. On Friday from noon to 9 pm, when to-dos are due Saturday to Monday and the evening has two or more unbooked hours, a "Free your weekend" group above "Start today" names them and offers "Work on them tonight" (a work date, never a new due date) or "Not this weekend".
- Moments: on days that matter, a photo becomes the header fading into the paper and the big number becomes the moment. No promo carousel, no counter, no X. The moments: a break coming up, the first day of term (classes added days ahead, none met yet), a heavy week, the last deadline of the week done, and the first quiet day after two or more exams; each has its own photo. An action on a moment does the thing (spread the work), never only opens another tab. Text never sits on the photo: it has fully faded into the paper before the moment's first line (`solidAt` on `PhotoHeader`), so any photo works in light and dark mode. Only the glass view toggle sits on it.
- Add: composer, recent-screenshots strip, two "start with" rows. Reading: progress line and rows filling in with New pills.
- Item detail: pushed; light title, large time pair, plain rows, Notes and Same week, primary pill, destructive text button.
- Overlaps: agenda titles and status times use the conflict ink. The day bar marks the overlapping interval in red even in an item's highlighted view; hourly conflicts use plain terracotta blocks with contrasting text in separate columns, a 4px gap, and no outside stripe or border. Taller blocks put Overlaps below the time; compact blocks keep the title and time on one line. Below the detail graph, a named warning links to each conflicting event. Fixed events point toward the flexible plan to review. Plus offers an explicit move button with a calendar-free time and a 15-minute buffer; nothing moves automatically. Plan's day carries the same button under its list when that day has an upcoming flexible event overlapping another, naming both and keeping the other where it is; overlaps between fixed plans get no button. An event with no end holds an hour for overlaps, as the charts draw it, and an end equal to its start counts as no end. Overlap warnings, the flexibility switch, and manual edits stay free.
- You: "You" as the main heading with "Profile and preferences" above it, then the plan/import pill. Name and field of study belong in the Profile rows. Hairline rows in groups, footer with mark, version, legal links.
- Auth starts at `/auth`: wordmark, the serif line, one accent "Get started" pill into onboarding as a guest, and a muted "I have an account" link to log in (Oct 21, 2026, for conversion; `docs/conversion.md`). Apple, Google and email live on sign in and sign up. After a sign-out on this phone the welcome leads with "Continue as Ethan · Apple" (accent), "Get started" (grey) and "Not you? Remove"; sign in marks the provider last used. Only the first name, provider and a masked email are kept for it, never a token.
- Accounts have two states (Oct 21, 2026; research in `docs/conversion.md`). A guest's plan is only on this iPhone: You shows "Back up your plan", and the one destructive action is Erase plan (Your data), which also deletes the anonymous record the import limit created. Signed in, the plan is backed up: You shows "Backed up as <email>"; Sign out backs up, then removes the plan from this iPhone until the next sign-in (it refuses while offline), and Delete account removes the account, its backup and the plan here, revoking Sign in with Apple first. Signing in never merges: if this iPhone and the account both have a plan, a full-screen choice keeps one ("Keep account plan" / "Keep this iPhone's plan"). Wording is Apple's: Sign in, Sign out, Delete account; never Log in or Log out. Login, signup and forgot-password screens share labelled fields, password visibility and glass back chevrons. No preview badges or simulated successful authentication; unavailable identity actions explain the connection is not ready. Guest continues into onboarding (Oct 21, 2026, `docs/conversion.md`): "What's on your plate?" (Classes, A job, Clubs and practice; multi-select), "Where do your deadlines live?" (skipped for a job alone), their own import starting there (or Canvas, or a real labelled sample week), "Your term, at a glance" (facts from `termSummary`), "When should we remind you?", then Plus. Only Plus is a dark photo showcase; the rest is plain paper. No demo, no slideshows, no rating prompt.
