Researched Nov 1, 2026. Research and a proposed test only; Instagram posting remains deferred. The [TikTok queue](../design/social/README.md) stays separate. Earlier Instagram crops are archived and are not finished Instagram creative.

## Recommendation

Updated Nov 1 after the user questioned the faceless direction: **hold the twelve-post batch and start with a small creative pilot**. The references are creator-led; they do not establish that removing the student and retaining only desk footage or app screens will perform similarly. They also do not isolate whether showing a face caused the results.

Proposed first pilot: two real-student creator Reels showing genuine Semli use, plus two useful carousels. The founder stays off camera. Use the existing November creator budget only if those deliverables are still available within it; this proposal does not add spending or assume Instagram usage rights. Establish permission for the intended Instagram publication before using creator material. If suitable creator material is unavailable, keep Instagram deferred while the scheduled TikToks run.

The four-week, three-post cadence below is an earlier scale-up proposal to revisit after this pilot. It is not the next production commitment.

Make an Instagram version of the underlying student problem: a short demonstration, a checklist someone could use, or a joke someone would send to a classmate. A TikTok image resized for the feed does not establish that any of these formats will work.

This is a recommendation for a pilot, not a claim that we have found Semli's winning Instagram strategy. There are no Semli Instagram Insights in this research. Full competitor histories were inaccessible, so no account-specific posting cadence or best hour is verified.

## What the evidence actually says

| Source and period | Finding | Implication for Semli |
|---|---|---|
| [Metricool's Instagram study announcement](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/), June 16, 2026; 24,364,803 posts from 375,118 accounts, comparing Jan–Feb 2025 with Jan–Feb 2026 | Reels generated over four times single-image interactions; carousels generated nine times single-image saves. Instagram carousels also performed differently from TikTok carousels in its comparison | Test both Reels and carousels. Do not transfer TikTok's format decision to Instagram |
| [Metricool study overview](https://metricool.com/instagram-research-study/) | Carousel publishing increased about 24% and Reel publishing about 35% in its sample | These are established, competitive formats. Using one is not itself a differentiator |
| [Fanpage Karma format study](https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/instagram-format-reach/), over 3 million posts from Jan–Apr 2026 | For accounts below 10,000 followers, Reels led normalized reach and reached nearly three times as many non-followers as carousels. Single images led its interaction-rate comparison in that size band | Reels are worth testing for discovery. Keep single-image jokes available as a later experiment rather than declaring them dead |
| [Buffer's 2026 engagement report](https://buffer.com/resources/state-of-social-media-engagement-2026/) | Its separate 4M+ post format analysis found greater Reel reach and greater carousel engagement. That analysis uses Jan 2022–Oct 2024, despite appearing in a 2026 report | Directional support for testing both; older evidence, not a current platform-wide benchmark |
| [Buffer frequency study](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/), published Aug 13, 2025, now titled for 2026 | About 2.1 million posts from 102,000 accounts. Posting 3–5 times weekly was associated with about 12% more reach per post than 1–2 times. Photos, Reels and carousels were pooled | Three weekly posts is a reasonable workload-conscious starting point. It does not prove three is optimal or establish a Reel quota |
| [Meta's January 2026 update](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/01/2026-ai-drives-performance/) | Meta reported that 75% of US Instagram recommendations were coming from original posts after changes in Q4 2025 | Use Semli's own recordings, screens and writing. The reported share is not a guarantee of recommendations |

These studies use different samples, periods, denominators and account-size bands. Total interactions, interaction rate, views and unique reach are not interchangeable. A ninefold save total does not mean ninefold save rate. None of these studies establishes which format produces Semli installs or what happens for an account with almost no followers.

Metricool's [press release](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/) explicitly recommends Reels for new audiences, carousels for material worth saving, and Stories for audience connection. That is a useful starting framework. It does not make Stories incapable of discovery or carousels incapable of reaching non-followers.

## Earlier scale-up proposal — on hold

After the smaller pilot, consider **six Reels and six carousels over four weeks**. These are creative hypotheses, not completed assets or approved captions.

| Topic | Reel version | Carousel version |
|---|---|---|
| Shift and class collide | Show two sample inputs and the visible overlap in one plan; move straight to the conflict | Four frames: the shift, the class, the overlap, what to check before agreeing to a shift |
| Dates hidden in a syllabus | Show one sample syllabus, the review screen and the resulting dates | A short checklist of what to check: exams, recurring deadlines, exceptions and changed dates |
| The weekly reset | Show the actual steps to review next week's classes, shifts and deadlines | A checklist that is useful without downloading Semli, with one app example |
| A deadline changes | Record the actual supported update flow, including the person's review | Explain what changed, where to check, and which date to confirm; avoid implying automatic updates unless demonstrated |
| The group project | A short, clearly fictional sample schedule with one readable joke | A short visual joke or sequence; every frame must add something rather than stretch one sentence |
| Too much in one day | Show a deliberately crowded sample day and inspect the clash | A practical weekly-planning example with time left for travel and meals; no health or academic-outcome claims |

For Reels, try roughly 10–20 seconds as a production starting point, then adjust for readability and retention. That range is not a proven optimal duration. Use our own clean 9:16 masters, real interactions, and legible native text. Holding a TikTok screenshot for eight seconds is a fallback export, not the proposed Instagram Reel format.

For carousels, try four to six frames: a specific first frame, useful steps or a clear progression, and a final frame that completes the idea. Rebuild for the feed using real screens and restrained text. The poor early TikTok slide result does not establish that Instagram carousels will fail; the proposed Instagram carousel also needs to earn each swipe.

One complete caption per post. A joke can have one line. A tutorial can include the detail needed to use it. Do not add a second explanation of the punchline, generic motivational language, forced questions or a repeated hashtag block. Use a call to action only when it is useful, such as saving an actual checklist. Caption length and hashtag count are not being treated as growth rules.

## Posting cadence and times

The cadence in this section applies only if the smaller creative pilot justifies a larger batch and there is capacity to run it. Producing three additional weekly posts competes with the existing marketing time budget; it is not free because some source footage already exists.

[Buffer's September 2026 timing study](https://buffer.com/resources/when-is-the-best-time-to-post-on-instagram/) analyzed 9.6 million posts, with data from Jan 2024–Dec 2025, and highlighted Thursday 9 am and Wednesday noon/6 pm. [Sprout's March 2026 study](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-instagram/) recommends broader windows including Tuesday 1–7 pm and Thursday noon–2 pm. Its research base of nearly 2 billion engagements and 307,000 profiles is cross-network, not Instagram-only.

For a manageable first run, use **Tuesday 1 pm, Thursday noon, Sunday 6 pm, America/New_York**. Tuesday and Thursday are within Sprout's reported windows. Sunday is a deliberate weekly-planning hypothesis, not a claimed benchmark winner. Keep these slots fixed while testing creative so we are not trying to determine format and posting hour from twelve different posts at once.

| Week | Tuesday 1 pm | Thursday noon | Sunday 6 pm |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reel | Carousel | Reel |
| 2 | Carousel | Reel | Carousel |
| 3 | Carousel | Reel | Carousel |
| 4 | Reel | Carousel | Reel |

Each format gets six posts and two appearances in each weekday slot. Rotate comparable topics too. This is a small exploratory test, not a randomized experiment or a clean timing test. After choosing a workable creative mix, test hours using Instagram's own follower activity and post results.

Stories: on two days, use a short follow-up to a feed post, a genuine question, or a link to the current destination. Check replies and link taps. Do not add daily filler to meet a quota. Keep the short-lived question or result fresh instead of queueing fake interactions weeks ahead.

## What to measure

Read posts at 72 hours and seven days, recording the actual observation age if the Sunday review is late. Keep format and organic/paid status separate. Use per-post medians, and retain the individual results so one large Reel does not hide a weak series.

| Question | Measures |
|---|---|
| Did new people see it? | Unique accounts reached, non-follower reach where available, follows attributed to the post where available |
| Would someone pass it along? | Shares per 100 accounts reached; keep likes and comments separately |
| Was it useful enough to keep? | Saves per 100 accounts reached |
| Did the Reel hold attention? | Average watch time, duration, retention/skip information where available; compare similar lengths |
| Did it create product interest? | Attributed profile visits where available, weekly `?r=ig` bio clicks and linked outcomes where measurable |

Shares are not necessarily a separate count of private DM sends. Views can include repeat viewing; do not substitute them for unique reach without labeling that change. If a field is unavailable, leave it blank. Account-wide bio clicks do not attribute an install to a particular post.

Use this test to decide what is worth making again, not to declare a platform rule. A practical follow-up signal is a format consistently leading on its intended measure across several topics, with enough actual shares/saves to interpret. If the result is one outlier or almost all zeros, keep testing or change the idea. Likes and followers alone do not establish customer acquisition.

## Batching and publishing constraints

Prepare source footage and finished carousel frames in a separate Instagram folder. A simple initial stockpile is six Reel masters, six carousel sets and their captions. Budget a separate production session for that stockpile; after it exists, aim for a 30–45 minute Sunday review/queue pass, plus the time to replace used drafts. Those are planning estimates, not measured production times.

Meta's [Trial Reels announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/) describes showing a Reel to non-followers first, with early metrics around 24 hours later. Its [June 2025 update](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/06/inspiring-creativity-that-brings-people-together/) expanded availability. Check Semli's actual account controls. If used, record Trial Reels separately from the ordinary Reel/carousel pilot; they are not a randomized A/B tool.

Native scheduling and audio controls need an account check. The [Instagram scheduling help page](https://help.instagram.com/439971288310029/) was rate-limited during research. Third-party guides commonly report a 75-day horizon, but I have not verified it in Semli's composer. A rolling seven-day queue avoids making the workflow depend on that advertised maximum.

The earlier Post Bridge inventory did not include Semli. Its tools expose Instagram scheduling, carousel media and Reel options, but account connection and a read-back/publication check are still needed. No posts were created.

Use music with rights suitable for this brand use, keeping an unscored master. The [Instagram music-library help page](https://help.instagram.com/402084904469945/) redirected to login, so I could not verify its current account-specific guidance. Later's trend list itself cautions that “original audio” does not mean royalty-free and points to [Meta Sound Collection](https://www.facebook.com/sound/collection/). A track appearing in a trend list or another creator's Reel is not evidence that Semli can use it.

Keep sample student materials fictional or appropriately cleared, show actual product behavior, and verify release status and the acquisition destination before writing download claims. The current repository still records acquisition as going to the beta waitlist; a planned January release is not a current availability claim.

## What this research has not established

Semli's Instagram baseline, a winning competitor cadence, the best posting hour for students, conversion lift from a particular format, and whether the current proposed jokes are good enough remain unknown. The strongest next evidence would be Semli's own Insights and a complete recent feed audit of several small college/nursing accounts, including their ordinary posts. Instagram posting remains deferred while that separate strategy is evaluated.
