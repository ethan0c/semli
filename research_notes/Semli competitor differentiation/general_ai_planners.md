# General-purpose daily planners and AI scheduling tools students use instead of a student planner (as of Oct 30, 2026, US)

Method note: about 20 searches and fetches. 9to5mac.com, macrumors.com and temporal.day could not be fetched (blocked in this session's network), so claims from those outlets rest on search-result snippets only. Most pricing comes from third-party review/affiliate blogs that often disagree with each other; treat every price as "verify on the vendor's paywall". Many 2026 "honest answer" posts come from competing vendors (Carly, Morgen, Lifestack, Temporal, Photo2Calendar), which have a reason to downplay incumbents.

## 1. Which do AI auto-scheduling, natural-language/voice capture, screenshot or document import, and recurring events in the free tier?

### Takeaway
Auto-scheduling (putting tasks into open time on the calendar) is still a paid, work-oriented feature (Motion, Reclaim, Akiflow, Sunsama, Morgen). Voice and natural-language capture is now common and partly free (Todoist Ramble, 10 sessions a month on free; Tiimo; Claude on iOS). Screenshot-to-event is now built into the OS and the general chatbots (Apple Visual Intelligence, Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude). None of the sources describe a tool that combines classes, work shifts and deadlines with overlap detection and a review step, the way Semli does.

### Cited Findings
**Auto-scheduling, all paid:**
- Motion: Pro AI at $27/mo, or $19/mo billed yearly. Business AI at $45/mo, or $29/mo billed yearly. — [Ellie Planner](https://ellieplanner.com/productivity-copilot/motion-app-pricing); another source lists $19 and $29 per seat billed monthly — [get-alfred](https://get-alfred.ai/blog/motion-pricing). No permanent free plan, and the App Store prices reportedly disagree with the website. — [meetergo / aggregated search](https://meetergo.com/en/magazine/reclaim-vs-motion-vs-meetergo)
- Reclaim.ai has a free Lite plan. Starter is about $8 to $10 per user per month billed annually, Business $12 and Enterprise $18. — [hackceleration](https://hackceleration.com/labs/reclaim-pricing); [Morgen](https://www.morgen.so/blog-posts/reclaim-ai-alternatives). Dropbox owns Reclaim (bought 2024). A native iOS app is listed as "In Planning" on the public roadmap as of mid-2026. — [archcalendar](https://blog.archcalendar.com/6-best-reclaim-ai-alternatives-in-2026-now-that-dropbox-owns-it/); [WorkifyLab](https://workifylab.com/best-calendar-apps-2026/)
- Akiflow: $34/mo billed monthly, or $19/mo billed annually. — [Ellie Planner](https://ellieplanner.com/comparisons/akiflow-alternative)
- Sunsama: $25/mo or $20/mo billed annually per a March 2026 article. An August 2026 review says $22/mo or $17/mo billed yearly. — [Morgen](https://www.morgen.so/blog-posts/sunsama-vs-akiflow); [Akiflow blog](https://akiflow.com/blog/sunsama-vs-reclaimai) (sources conflict)
- Clockwise, a Reclaim rival, was acquired by Salesforce and shut down in March 2026. — [WorkifyLab](https://workifylab.com/best-calendar-apps-2026/)

**Voice and natural-language capture:**
- Todoist Ramble is voice-to-task and launched January 2026. The free (Beginner) plan includes 10 Ramble sessions a month. Pro is $5/mo billed annually or $7 billed monthly. The free plan is capped at 5 active projects. — [Asian Efficiency](https://www.asianefficiency.com/task-management/todoist-ai-setup-guide/); [Morgen](https://www.morgen.so/blog-posts/todoist-pricing)
- Tiimo's AI Co-Planner turns a typed or spoken brain-dump into a structured plan. Free tier: "basic planner, limited AI chats, 1 profile." — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/tiimo/id1480220328); [Lifestack](https://lifestack.ai/blog/tiimo-pricing)
- Claude on iOS, all plans, Aug 25, 2026: it can read the calendar to find free time, create events, create reminders, add to lists, and manage recurring reminders. The article does not mention alarms. — [Anthropic support](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11869619-use-claude-with-ios-apps)

**Screenshot or document import:** see question 3.

**Free tiers with calendar or recurring basics:**
- TickTick has a free tier. Premium is about $35.99/yr. One review says it has "no AI assistance for planning." — [checkthat.ai](https://checkthat.ai/brands/ticktick/pricing); [Lifestack](https://lifestack.ai/blog/app-to-use-with-ticktick)
- Notion Calendar is free but works only with Google Calendar, with no AI daily planning. — [Morgen](https://www.morgen.so/blog-posts/best-ai-calendar-apps)
- Things 3 is a one-time purchase: $9.99 on iPhone, $49.99 on Mac. It has no AI. — [nerdynav](https://nerdynav.com/ticktick-vs-things-3/)
- Fantastical is about $5.99 to $6.99/mo, positioned on design and Apple integration rather than AI. — [toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/fantastical/pricing)
- Structured Pro: AI planning and calendar sync are behind Pro. — [toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/structured)
- Amie now leads with AI meeting notes (a shift dating from Feb 2025). It shipped Outlook sync and Gmail integration on Aug 5, 2026. — [aggregated search; WorkifyLab / archcalendar](https://workifylab.com/best-calendar-apps-2026/) (single thin source)

### Inferences
- The auto-schedulers are priced and designed for knowledge workers with meetings ($17 to $34/mo), not for students with fixed class blocks and shifts. Semli's Plus at $4.99/mo is far cheaper. Semli's rule-based rescheduling is a lighter version of their core feature.
- Voice and natural-language task capture is now table stakes. Todoist gives it away in limited form, so it can't be a headline differentiator for Semli.
- The pieces Semli combines did not show up together in any substitute: class schedules, work shifts and deadlines in one plan, overlap or conflict detection across them, and alarms that ring through silent mode for urgent items.

### Gaps
- I did not verify Routine, Llama Life or Microsoft To Do/Copilot pricing or features this round (no searches budgeted). Treat them as unresearched.
- Morgen and Amie pricing were not found.
- I found no source confirming whether any of these apps uses iOS AlarmKit for alarms that ring through silent mode. This is worth checking directly in the Structured and Tiimo release notes.

## 2. How cheap or free are they for students?

### Takeaway
The big platform players are effectively free for US students in 2026. Google gives US college students a free year of AI Pro (Gemini in Gmail, Calendar and Docs) if they sign up by Dec 31, 2026. Notion gives a free Plus workspace. Claude's iOS calendar and reminder actions work on every plan. Dedicated AI schedulers mostly don't publish student pricing; Motion's and Akiflow's student discounts are unadvertised or contested.

### Cited Findings
- Google AI Pro is free for a year for eligible US college students who sign up by Dec 31, 2026. Students must be 18 or older, use a personal Google account and verify a school email. Workspace for Education accounts are excluded. After the year it renews at $19.99/mo. Students in 140+ other markets get AI Plus instead. — [MacRumors, Aug 19, 2026 (snippet; page blocked)](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/19/students-free-year-google-ai-pro/); [techjacksolutions](https://techjacksolutions.com/ai-tools/google-gemini/google-gemini-student/); [opentherank](https://opentherank.com/ai-pricing/gemini/student-discount/)
- The older "Gemini Advanced free through finals 2026" offer from 2025 has expired. — [aifreeapi](https://www.aifreeapi.com/en/posts/gemini-student-plan)
- Notion for Education gives a free Plus workspace to students at colleges listed in WHED who have a school email. K-12 students are not eligible. Notion Calendar and Notion Mail are free on any plan. — [Notion educators](https://www.notion.com/educators); [Lifestack](https://lifestack.ai/blog/notion-pricing). Sources conflict on AI for students: one says 20 trial responses, another says no AI, another says 50% off the AI add-on, and another says the standalone add-on was removed in 2026. — [justbeingresourceful](https://justbeingresourceful.com/2026/04/11/how-students-can-get-notion-plus-for-free-and-notion-ai-at-half-price/); [flowith](https://flowith.io/blog/notion-ai-free-tips-teams-students)
- Motion's student discount is contested. The current public pricing page shows none, per two recent 2026 reviews. — [toolguidehq](https://toolguidehq.com/is-motion-worth-it-for-college-students-2026/). G2 and get-alfred say verified students get 25% off. — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/motionapp/pricing); [get-alfred](https://get-alfred.ai/blog/motion-pricing). The 2023-24 blogs claiming 50% off are likely stale. — [Nathan Cashion](https://www.nathancashion.com/blog/2023/9/this-ai-productivity-tool-helps-me-manage-my-time-as-a-graduate-student)
- Akiflow's pricing page invites students to get in touch, with no published amount. One reviewer reports a student discount granted within an hour. — [softsnoop](https://softsnoop.com/blog/akiflow-review/)
- I found no published student discount for Reclaim or Sunsama. Reclaim has a free Lite tier. — [Morgen](https://www.morgen.so/blog-posts/reclaim-ai-alternatives)
- Structured Pro is listed at $6.49/mo, $19.99/yr or $64.99 lifetime in most 2026 sources. One review checked Sep 10, 2026 gives US prices of $6.99/mo, $29.99/yr or $99.99 lifetime. Prices vary by region. — [toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/structured); [habitbox](https://habitbox.app/blog/structured-app-review) (conflict)
- Tiimo's price varies by source: $7.99/mo or $79.99/yr; about $54.99/yr; about $10/mo; $7 to $12 depending on region. The 7-day trial applies only to yearly plans. — [Lifestack](https://lifestack.ai/blog/tiimo-pricing); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiimo); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/tiimo/id1480220328)
- Todoist Pro is $5/mo billed annually. TickTick Premium is about $36/yr. Things 3 is a one-time purchase. Notion Calendar is free. (Sources in question 1.)

### Inferences
- On price, Semli's student plan ($24.99/yr) sits close to Structured ($20 to $30/yr), Todoist (~$60/yr) and TickTick (~$36/yr). It is far below Motion, Akiflow and Sunsama ($200 to $400/yr). The real price competitor is $0: Gemini via the Google AI Pro student year, Claude's free tier, and the free Apple Intelligence features on newer iPhones.
- Google's free AI Pro year ends Dec 31, 2026 for sign-ups. Students who claimed it hit $19.99/mo renewals during 2027. That is a possible moment to reach them, but this is speculative.

### Gaps
- None of these vendors' own pricing pages were fetched, so every price is secondhand. Several sources explicitly conflict.
- ChatGPT's student pricing for 2026 was not researched.

## 3. How far have Apple, Google and OpenAI gone toward "read this and put it on my calendar"?

### Takeaway
This is real and shipping. Since iOS 26, a screenshot on an Apple Intelligence iPhone (15 Pro or later) shows an "Add to Calendar" button that pulls one event out of the screenshot, with an editable preview. Gemini can take a photo or screenshot of a schedule and, after a preview and confirmation, create events in Google Calendar. Claude on iOS can create events and recurring reminders on all plans. ChatGPT's Google Calendar app reportedly gained write access (create only) in 2026. What none of them were documented doing is the rest of Semli's job: whole-semester recurring class and shift structure, conflict detection across classes and shifts, and ongoing reminders and alarms tied to deadlines.

### Cited Findings
**Apple (iOS 26):**
- When a screenshot contains event details, Visual Intelligence shows "Add to Calendar". Tapping it opens an editable preview, and "Create Event" adds it. Detailed editing hands off to the Calendar app. — [gHacks, Mar 6, 2026](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/03/06/ios-26-lets-you-instantly-add-calendar-events-from-screenshots/); [9to5Mac, Mar 5, 2026 (snippet; page blocked)](https://9to5mac.com/2026/03/05/ios-26-gives-apples-calendar-app-a-convenient-new-advantage/); [iDrop News](https://www.idropnews.com/ios-26/ios-26-screenshot-calendar-events/256322/)
- It requires Apple Intelligence: iPhone 15 Pro, the 16 series, and the 17 series including iPhone Air. — [MacRumors how-to (snippet)](https://www.macrumors.com/how-to/use-visual-intelligence-screen-content/)
- The button appears only when an event is detected. A third-party blog claims it can save to any calendar, including Google (unverified). — [Photo2Calendar](https://photo2calendar.com/ios-26-screenshot-to-calendar) (vendor with an interest)
- I found no source saying it handles a whole syllabus or multi-page PDF, or sets up recurring classes from one capture.

**Google:**
- Gemini app flow: connect Google Calendar, type @Google Calendar, attach a photo or screenshot, and Gemini previews the extracted events. Reply "Yes" and they are created. The Calendar actions reportedly target the default calendar only and leave some actions unsupported (per Google's help page, cited secondhand). Photos read better than image PDFs. — [note.com teacher guide, Feb 2026](https://note.com/kouteijyutu/n/n224a6d467a4c?hl=en); [Photo2Calendar](https://photo2calendar.com/how-to-use-google-gemini-add-events-from-photo); [n8n workflow](https://n8n.io/workflows/18986-plan-study-sessions-from-syllabus-pdfs-with-gemini-google-calendar-and-sheets/)
- Gmail added a Gemini-powered "Add to calendar" button in March 2025. — [TechCrunch, Mar 10, 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/10/gmail-gains-an-add-to-calendar-button-powered-by-gemini)
- The Gemini side panel in Calendar (Mar 2025) can check your schedule, create events and look up details. — [TechCrunch, Mar 7, 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/07/google-adds-a-gemini-panel-to-calendar-to-help-you-manage-your-schedule/)
- "Help me schedule" in Gmail (Oct 2025) suggests time slots from your calendar and the email's context. It covers two-person meetings only. — [Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2025/10/help-me-schedule-meeting-gmail-calendar.html); [TechCrunch, Oct 14, 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/14/googles-gemini-can-now-help-you-schedule-google-calendar-meetings)
- The Calendar side panel is reportedly still rolling out via Workspace Labs as of mid-2026. — [Carly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/google-calendar-ai-features/) (competitor blog; low confidence)

**OpenAI:**
- 2026 third-party sources say ChatGPT's Google Calendar app (Settings → Apps) now has read-write access and can create events. Moving or deleting events is undocumented. Calendar events don't trigger anything in ChatGPT. — [Carly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/chatgpt-google-calendar-integration/); [Carly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/can-chatgpt-access-google-calendar/). An older Cronofy post says the app is read-only. — [Cronofy](https://www.cronofy.com/blog/chatgpt-calendar-connectors). I found no official OpenAI help page confirming either.

**Anthropic:**
- Claude on iOS (all plans): it creates calendar events, finds free time, and creates recurring reminders. — [Anthropic support, Aug 25, 2026](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11869619-use-claude-with-ios-apps)
- Claude's Google Calendar connector docs say it is read-only: no creating or changing events. — [Claude docs](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/google/calendar)

**Reclaim inside ChatGPT:**
- Dropbox is launching a Reclaim calendar app inside ChatGPT. — [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/dropbox-chatgpt-apps-files-dash)

### Inferences
- For a single flyer, email or screenshot, Semli's "import" is already commoditized on iOS 26 for owners of an iPhone 15 Pro or later, and in Gemini for anyone. What isn't commoditized is:
  - turning a full syllabus into many dated deadlines plus recurring class meetings, through a deliberate review step;
  - reconciling that with irregular work shifts;
  - detecting conflicts between them;
  - the escalation behavior: reminders, then alarms that ring through silent mode for urgent items.
- Owners of older iPhones (pre-15 Pro) get no Apple Visual Intelligence. That is a meaningful share of college students on hand-me-down phones, though I found no data on how large.
- Chatbots create events but don't own the plan afterward. Nothing re-plans when a shift moves, and nothing nags. That gap is Semli's natural position.

### Gaps
- I could not reach Apple's own documentation or the 9to5Mac and MacRumors pages. Whether iOS 26 handles multiple events from one screenshot, and how iOS 26.x behaves, is unverified.
- No WWDC 2026 or iOS 27 calendar and AI announcements were researched. This needs checking because it is likely the biggest near-term threat.
- No Google I/O 2026 Calendar announcements were found. The Gemini 4 status in Calendar rests on one competitor blog.
- No official OpenAI documentation was found for ChatGPT's calendar write access or for ChatGPT Tasks.

## 4. Recent review sentiment, controversies and momentum

### Takeaway
Momentum is with the platforms and with the visual-planner indies (Tiimo won Apple's iPhone App of the Year for 2025). The work-oriented AI schedulers are consolidating: Clockwise shut down, Reclaim was absorbed into Dropbox, and Amie pivoted to meeting notes. Complaints about specific apps are mostly about price and paywalls rather than a single scandal. I found no hard evidence of a "Tiimo AI backlash" controversy.

### Cited Findings
- Tiimo won Apple's iPhone App of the Year in Dec 2025 (finalist in 2024). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiimo)
- Tiimo's AI sentiment is mixed. Some users find the suggestions generic and need extra time to fix them. — [woshipm.com analysis](https://www.woshipm.com/ai/6305974.html)
- An earlier Tiimo backlash (late 2024) was about a routines overhaul, not AI. A user of almost 5 years threatened to cancel. — [apptail](https://apptail.io/app/tiimo-to-do-list-ai-planner-nYs/canada)
- Tiimo announced a major Android rebuild on May 4, 2026. — [aiinsightsnews](https://aiinsightsnews.net/tiimo/)
- Structured moved to subscription pricing in 2025 with regional pricing. Long-time users who expected a one-time license are confused, the free tier is "too limited to evaluate," and Android has fewer features. — [temporal.day (snippet)](https://temporal.day/blog/best-structured-alternatives-2026); [toolguide](https://toolguide.io/en/tool/structured/); [habitbox](https://habitbox.app/blog/structured-app-review)
- Motion is a "top pick" in 2026 roundups, but its price is debated and it has no free version. — [content.fans](https://content.fans/news/motions-ai-calendar-tops-2026-productivity-but-price-debated)
- Clockwise shut down in March 2026. Reclaim is under Dropbox with unchanged pricing and still has no native iOS app. — [WorkifyLab](https://workifylab.com/best-calendar-apps-2026/); [archcalendar](https://blog.archcalendar.com/6-best-reclaim-ai-alternatives-in-2026-now-that-dropbox-owns-it/)

### Inferences
- Tiimo is the closest indie competitor in feel: a visual, iOS-first, award-winning app with an AI co-planner and a student-friendly look. It is aimed at neurodivergent day-structuring, not at managing courses and shifts.
- Structured's paywall friction is something Semli can use: keep the free tier clearly useful so students can evaluate it.
- Consolidation among the work-focused AI calendars suggests that standalone "AI auto-schedule" is a hard business. Semli is better off not positioning on auto-scheduling.

### Gaps
- I found no Reddit threads directly. The sentiment above comes from review aggregators and blogs.
- App Store ratings and review counts for each competitor were not collected.
- No data was found on download numbers or revenue for Structured, Tiimo or Motion.
- Not covered: Routine, Llama Life, Microsoft To Do/Copilot, Apple Reminders' Apple Intelligence suggestions, and ChatGPT Tasks.
