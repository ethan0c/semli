## Which dedicated student apps do AI syllabus import?

### Takeaway
The internal claim that "nobody in this tier does AI import from screenshots and syllabi" is false and well out of date. By Oct 2026 there are at least 12 dedicated iOS student apps or web tools that turn a syllabus PDF or photo into dates, many of them launched in 2025–2026. Several say they show a review step before saving (Syllabot, ClassPulse, SyllaScan, Semora not confirmed), and some sit in Semli's price band or below it (ClassPulse $5.99/semester, SyllabAI $3.99/mo, SyllabusAI $6.99/mo, Syllabus AI $12.99/yr). What still looks thin is one app that combines syllabus, work-shift screenshot and message import with a review step that shows evidence for each item and an undo for the whole import.

### Inferences
- The App Store has filled up with single-purpose "AI syllabus scanner" apps. Several App IDs are 675xxxxxxx–680xxxxxxx, which points to late 2025 through 2026 launches (inferred from ID ranges only). Being able to read a syllabus is now table stakes, not a differentiator.
- A review/confirm step is common too (Syllabot, ClassPulse, SyllaScan, Apple Calendar, Gemini, Todoist all show a preview). What competitors claim less often is evidence for each item (the source line) and undoing an import as a unit. DormWay's "source-backed" language is the closest overlap found.
- On price, Semli's $4.99/mo is below Shovel and UpAhead but above SyllabAI's $3.99/mo, and its $24.99/yr is above ClassPulse's $17.99/yr and Syllabus AI's $12.99/yr. Free tools (DormWay, ChatGPT, Gemini, SyllabusAI free) cap what Semli can charge for import alone.

## Which web tools, Chrome extensions and GPTs do syllabus to .ics for free?

### Takeaway
Several free or freemium web tools turn a syllabus into a downloadable .ics file, along with Chrome text-to-event extensions and open-source projects. Universities also teach students to do it with ChatGPT. I found no specific popular custom GPT.

### Cited findings
- **AI Syllabus to Calendar Converter** (syllabus-extractor-widget.vercel.app): takes a PDF, PNG or JPG and exports ICS or JSON. — [site](https://syllabus-extractor-widget.vercel.app/)
- **Scholarly AI Calendar Generator**: syllabus or schedule to .ics. The free plan gives 1 AI credit in total. Its guide says no mainstream calendar app reads a syllabus PDF and pulls out the dates, and that the .ics does not stay in sync, so changes mean regenerating and re-importing. — [scholarly.so tool](https://scholarly.so/tools/ai-calendar-generator); [scholarly.so guide](https://scholarly.so/blog/how-to-turn-a-syllabus-into-a-calendar-with-ai)
- **Taskade** web converter, "course syllabus to student calendar". — [Taskade](https://www.taskade.com/convert/education/course-syllabus-to-student-calendar)
- **text-2-ics.com**: images or screenshots to ICS using OCR and AI, plus a **Text-2-ICS Chrome extension** that pulls events out of any text. — [text-2-ics blog](https://www.text-2-ics.com/blog/convert-screenshot-to-calendar-events-automatically); [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/text-2-ics-extension/aepojghkdjlgakgcehjljgdhajbdigej)
- **text-to-cal.com**: a free ICS generator from text, and its Chrome extension turns highlighted text into Google or Outlook events. — [text-to-cal](https://www.text-to-cal.com/tools/ics-generator)
- **Open source**: GitHub "Syllabus-Converter-" turns syllabi into a confirmed schedule, with a review step beside the source PDF, and exports ICS. Self-hosted. — [GitHub](https://github.com/520HXC/Syllabus-Converter-)
- **n8n workflow template**: syllabus PDF in Drive, then Gemini, then Google Calendar events and a Sheets log (for developers). — [n8n](https://n8n.io/workflows/18986-plan-study-sessions-from-syllabus-pdfs-with-gemini-google-calendar-and-sheets/)

### Inferences
- A student comfortable with tech has several $0 routes. Semli's paid value has to come from trust (review, evidence, undo), keeping things up to date, and planner context, not from extraction itself.

### Gaps
- Usage numbers for these tools, and whether they are maintained, were not verified. No popular "syllabus to calendar" custom GPT was identified in search.

## Which general AI planners added screenshot or photo to event?

### Takeaway
Apple (iOS 26 Calendar and Visual Intelligence), Google (Gemini with Calendar) and Todoist (Task Capture, beta Apr 21, 2026) all now turn images into events or tasks with a preview step, and Tiimo has a photo-to-plan feature. A crowd of standalone "screenshot to calendar" apps exists as well. I found no evidence for Structured, and I did not get to Motion or Fantastical.

### Cited findings
- **Apple iOS 26**: a screenshot can become a Calendar event through Apple Intelligence, with a preview before "Create Event". It needs an Apple Intelligence-capable iPhone. Reported Mar 2026. — [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/03/05/ios-26-gives-apples-calendar-app-a-convenient-new-advantage/); [gHacks](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/03/06/ios-26-lets-you-instantly-add-calendar-events-from-screenshots/)
- **Google Gemini plus Calendar**: you attach a schedule image, Gemini previews the events and asks for confirmation. Reports say it can only write to the account's **default** calendar. A Japanese teacher's guide says it reads smartphone photos much more accurately than PDFs, and recommends asking it to OCR scanned PDFs first. — [photo2calendar](https://photo2calendar.com/how-to-use-google-gemini-add-events-from-photo); [scalevise](https://scalevise.com/resources/gemini-pdf-calendar-automation/); [note.com](https://note.com/kouteijyutu/n/n224a6d467a4c?hl=en); [techcoachjuarez (Dec 2025)](https://techcoachjuarez.com/2025/12/15/quick-scheduling-magic-using-gemini-and-google-calendar/)
- **Todoist**: Task Capture, in beta since **Apr 21, 2026**, scans photos, whiteboards, handwritten notes and printed documents (JPEG, PNG, WebP up to 10MB), plus screenshots and PDFs, into tasks **with a preview** before adding. Ramble is the voice-to-tasks feature. — [Todoist help](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/product-updates/scan-notes-photos-and-files-for-tasks-task-capture-beta-apr-21-2026-S5GOh7A0e); [Todoist AI tools](https://www.todoist.com/ai-tools)
- **Tiimo**: App Store release notes describe "snap to plan", where a photo becomes tasks. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tiimo-to-do-list-planner/id1480220328); [super-productivity blog](https://super-productivity.com/blog/best-adhd-task-management-apps-2026/)
- **Structured**: its AI can create and adjust tasks, but no photo or screenshot import was found. — [super-productivity blog](https://super-productivity.com/blog/best-adhd-task-management-apps-2026/)
- **Standalone screenshot-to-calendar apps**: ShotCal (on-device AI, Apple, Google and Outlook calendars), Screenshot to Calendar (screentocal.com, ICS or sync), PicCal (splits several events out of a group-chat screenshot), Smart Calendars AI (photo, screenshot or PDF), Nori (family events), CalendarBridge (email a screenshot). — [ShotCal](https://lauperlabs.com/shotcal); [screentocal](https://www.screentocal.com/); [PicCal](https://piccal.ai/guides/turn-screenshot-into-calendar-event/); [Smart Calendars](https://www.smartcalendars.ai/en/articles/photo-to-calendar-ocr); [Nori](https://heynori.com/screenshot-to-calendar); [CalendarBridge](https://calendarbridge.com/blog/beat-brain-rot-with-ai-that-turns-screenshots-and-links-into-calendar-events/)

### Inferences
- Built-in OS and assistant features (Apple, Gemini) make one-off screenshot-to-event free and close to zero friction on newer devices. They handle one event at a time and don't plan or track recurring imports, which leaves room for a planner that handles batch imports, recurring imports and tracking where each item came from.

### Gaps
- Motion, Fantastical, Notion AI, Goblin Tools and ChatGPT Tasks were not researched within the tool budget. The exact date Apple's screenshot-to-Calendar shipped (iOS 26 release vs. a point update) is not confirmed. The articles are from Mar 2026.

## Can a student just upload a syllabus to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and get an .ics file or Google Calendar events for free? How well does it work?

### Takeaway
Yes. Universities publish guides for doing this with ChatGPT (syllabus to CSV or .ics, then a manual import into Google Calendar), and Gemini can write events straight to the default Google Calendar after a confirm step. Whether ChatGPT's Google Calendar connector can create events directly is disputed. Reported failure modes are time zones, ambiguous date formats and PDF layout or scan quality. I found no first-hand Reddit accuracy reports.

### Cited findings
- **Farmingdale State College (Sep 2025)** tells students to have ChatGPT turn syllabus text into a CSV with Google Calendar's headers, then import it through Settings, then Import & export. — [Farmingdale](https://www.farmingdale.edu/news/ram-program/2025/2025-09-04-build-your-semester-calendar-with-chatgpt-google-calendar.shtml)
- ChatGPT's Google Calendar connector: fluentbooking says it is mainly read-only and that creating events needs Zapier, Make or an API. usecarly (mid-2026) says it now has read-write access and can create an invite when asked, while moving or deleting events is undocumented. Both are vendor blogs; no OpenAI primary source was found. — [fluentbooking](https://fluentbooking.com/articles/chatgpt-google-calendar-integration/); [usecarly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/chatgpt-google-calendar-integration/)
- Failure modes reported: a time-zone mismatch between the prompt and the calendar, garbled output that needs regenerating, and mixed date formats or missing AM/PM. — [Modern Mom Playbook](https://modernmomplaybook.substack.com/p/ai-powered-parenting-no-more-forgotten); [Medium](https://medium.com/@philipto168/how-i-use-chatgpt-to-create-ics-calendar-files-from-any-website-1aa40fdd593d)
- Gemini reads phone photos better than PDFs; scanned PDFs need OCR first. — [note.com](https://note.com/kouteijyutu/n/n224a6d467a4c?hl=en); [scalevise](https://scalevise.com/resources/gemini-pdf-calendar-automation/)
- AI extraction can miss or misread dates, so verifying events against the syllabus is recommended. — [DormWay blog](https://dormway.app/blog/ai-syllabus-parser-automating-your-academic-schedule-in-2026)

### Inferences
- The free general-purpose AI route is Semli's real baseline competitor. It works, but it is a one-shot file with no link back to the source, no undo and no updates. Semli's review step with evidence and its undo answer exactly those weaknesses.

### Gaps
- No Reddit threads or other first-hand accuracy reports were surfaced. Claude's own calendar connectors and the current state of OpenAI's Calendar connector need checking against first-party docs.

## Does anyone import a work-shift schedule screenshot and diff it against existing shifts?

### Takeaway
Several apps import shifts from a screenshot (The Shift, Shift2Cal, PicCal, Smart Calendars), but I found **no evidence that any of them diffs a new screenshot against shifts already saved**. PicCal's own guide tells users to rescan and delete moved shifts by hand. Diffing appears to be an open differentiator, with the caveat that this rests on missing evidence.

### Cited findings
- **The Shift: Work Schedule** (iOS and Android): you upload one screenshot and it extracts the start and end of each shift, shows them in widgets and exports them to your calendar. It processes everything locally on the device. App ID 6782529001 suggests a 2026 launch (inferred). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-shift-work-schedule/id6782529001)
- **Shift2Cal** (Android, web): AI scans a photo, screenshot or PDF into events, syncs with Google, Apple and Outlook, and supports recurring shifts and rotations. — [shift2cal.com](https://shift2cal.com/); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.loudsrl.shift2calendar)
- **PicCal** (iPhone): its guide for WorkJam schedules says that when the schedule changes, you "screenshot the new week and scan it again, then remove any shift that moved". In other words, it does no automatic diff. It also notes that some employers block screenshots on work phones. — [PicCal WorkJam guide](https://piccal.ai/guides/add-workjam-schedule-to-calendar/)
- **Smart Calendars**: turns screenshots, PDFs and rotas into reviewed shift events with recurrence and reminders. — [Smart Calendars](https://www.smartcalendars.ai/en/articles/shift-schedule-to-calendar)
- **Calfeed** has a guide on adding a work schedule to iPhone Calendar. — [Calfeed](https://calfeed.ai/learn/add-work-schedule-to-phone-calendar)

### Inferences
- Importing shifts from a screenshot is already common. Re-importing a week, detecting moved, added and dropped shifts, and showing the changes for review is not claimed by any product I found. Semli could own that, and it fits with undoing an import.

### Gaps
- I didn't check every shift app's changelog, so a diff feature might exist without showing up in search. No data was found on how many students use these shift apps.
