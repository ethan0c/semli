# Family calendar / AI schedule-import competition (US parents, as of Oct 30, 2026)

Research note on method: about 20 tool calls. Blocked in this session: apps.apple.com, play.google.com, reddit.com, and several vendor and roundup sites (getsense.ai, agent-finder.co, youthsportshq.substack.com). Most facts below come from search-result summaries of the cited pages, not full-page reads. Many sources are competitor blogs or affiliate roundups (heynori, getsense, usecarly, alphamothers, photo2calendar). Treat their claims about rivals as marketing, not verified fact. Play Store and App Store ratings could not be pulled directly.

## Q1. Who competes, and what does their AI import do? (competitor matrix)

### Takeaway
"Forward the school email or snap the flyer, and the AI makes the events" is now a commodity feature. It ships in the market leader (Skylight Sidekick/Magic Import, $79/yr), in Cozi's top tier, in Hearth, in Amazon Alexa+, natively in iOS (Visual Intelligence) and through Gemini, plus at least eight AI-first startups (Ohai, Nori, Sense, Fambot, Carly, Honeydew, Nuet, Jam). Two things are not clearly marketed by anyone I found: a per-item "where this was read" review step, and diffing a revised schedule against saved events.

### Inferences
- Photo, PDF and email-to-events extraction is table stakes. Big platforms give it away (Apple, Google, Amazon), and the leader bundles it into a $79/yr plan that ships with hardware. A standalone "AI import" pitch will not differentiate.
- Semli's possible wedges are features nobody prominently markets:
  - a per-item "read from here" review step (Skylight's model is import first, undo later);
  - a diff of a revised sports schedule against saved events (no one found);
  - true ringing alarms.
- These are workflow-quality differences, though, not category-creating ones.
- Price anchors: $40–$90/yr for family organizers, $60–$120/yr for AI assistants, $120–$480/yr for Ohai's premium tiers.
- iPhone-only is common among small AI entrants (Sense). Incumbents are cross-platform, and shared family calendars need the other parent, who may be on Android.

## Q4. What's missing, per reviews and press

### Takeaway
The pain is fragmentation: each team, school and platform has its own app and feed. Parents copy items by hand or lose them. Sports feeds come one team at a time and land as separate calendars. I found no evidence of any product that detects schedule changes and propagates them as diffs.

### Cited Findings
- Boston Globe (Oct 5, 2026): parents are overwhelmed by ParentSquare and dozens of other school and sports platforms. One parent can't tell her two sons' TeamSnap profiles apart ("b56-4" and "b56-1"). Another says she must copy items into her calendar immediately or lose them. — [Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/10/05/lifestyle/parents-schools-edtech-apps-platforms-passwords/)
- TeamSnap's calendar feed is one link per team and only reachable on the web. Three teams across three apps means three subscriptions. — [ScheduleCaddie](https://schedulecaddie.com/how-to/teamsnap-calendar-link/)
- Subscribed sports calendars appear as a separate calendar and don't merge into the Family Sharing calendar. — [Apple Community](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/253174724)
- ParentSquare reviews call it "extremely confusing". — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/198436/Clever-Synergy/reviews)
- Skylight complaints: paywalled basics, sync problems, and imports that "may not always get everything right". — [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1438779037/skylight-app/reviews); [Skylight Zendesk](https://skylight.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/18039340445211-How-To-Use-Skylight-Magic-Import)
- AI critique: worry that assistants raise pressure on mothers to deliver a perfect family experience. — [Fast Company](https://fastcompany.com/91599193/ai-wants-to-become-your-familys-chief-of-staff-should-you-let-it)

### Inferences
- "Revised schedule vs. saved events" (Semli's diff feature) maps directly onto the change-propagation pain. Incumbents frame imports as one-shot. This is the clearest differentiated angle found.
- Accuracy and trust are the reported weak points, so a review step that shows the source matches a real complaint.

### Gaps
- No direct review data on GameChanger, SportsEngine, ClassDojo, Remind or Bloomz sync problems (Reddit and app stores blocked).

## Q5. How crowded and well-funded is the space?

### Takeaway
It is very crowded and moderately funded. Money is concentrated in Skylight, a debt-financed, high-revenue hardware and subscription incumbent that already ships the exact AI import feature. Platform giants also give the feature away: Apple Visual Intelligence, Gemini and Alexa+. Below them are 10+ AI-first apps on a few million dollars each, and the category has notable failures (Milo, Yohana, Maple).

### Cited Findings
- See Q1 and Q2. Count of players found offering AI import of email, photos or PDFs into a family calendar:
  - incumbents and hardware: Skylight, Hearth, Cozi Max;
  - platforms: Amazon Alexa+, Apple, Google Gemini;
  - startups: Ohai, Nori, Sense, Fambot, Carly, Honeydew, Nuet, Jam;
  - photo-to-calendar utilities: Capture2Calendar, EventSnap+, photo2calendar, piccal.

### Inferences
- A broad "AI family calendar" pivot would enter a red ocean against a well-capitalized leader and free OS features.
- More defensible: a narrow wedge such as schedule-change tracking for multi-team sports families, with a source-highlighted review step and loud alarms. But that wedge depends on the other parent's platform, and Semli has no Android version.
- These are inferences, not validated demand.

### Gaps
- No independent market-size or retention data for any player.
- Skylight's 2025–2026 revenue is unverified.
