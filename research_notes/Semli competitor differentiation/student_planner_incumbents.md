Method note: apps.apple.com, mystudylife.com and dormway.app could not be fetched directly in this session (network allowlist). Many roundups are written by competitors (DormWay, MyStudyLife, Coursicle, Power Planner, StudyWillow, PlanWiz, Ellie); their bias is flagged inline.

## Feature-and-price matrix per incumbent

### Takeaway
The category splits into (a) long-running free or cheap timetable/homework planners (MyStudyLife, Power Planner, myHomework, Egenda, iStudiez, School Planner) that mostly lack LMS sync and AI, and (b) newer LMS-sync and AI-syllabus planners (Shovel, Coursicle, DormWay, Feymark, MyStudyLife's newer AI tier). Timetable, rotating schedules, assignments/exams and reminders are table stakes; LMS sync and AI syllabus import are now common among the newer US entrants; no incumbent found combines work shifts with school or offers ringing alarms.

### Inferences
- Table stakes for Semli: free calendar/timetable, repeating and rotating classes, assignments/exams, reminders. Every incumbent has these, mostly free (Power Planner and myHomework sell upgrades for about $5).
- Common among newer US competitors, so no longer rare: LMS sync (Shovel, Coursicle, myHomework Canvas import) and AI syllabus import (Shovel, Coursicle (disputed), Feymark, DormWay). Semli's iCal-feed approach matches what students can do without school API approval. Shovel uses deeper multi-LMS sync, but its reviews complain about server reliance and no offline mode.
- Rare or absent: (1) work shifts treated as first-class items with work-versus-school overlap detection (MyStudyLife only flags class-exam conflicts; Feymark only mentions part-time work); (2) a review step before AI-imported items are saved (no evidence any competitor advertises one); (3) ringing, alarm-style alerts for urgent items (none found); (4) local-first data (Shovel reviewers ask for offline mode). These look like Semli's real differentiators.
- Price position: Semli Plus at $4.99/mo or $24.99/yr is below Shovel ($9.79/mo, $35/yr) and MyStudyLife ($29.99-39.99/yr), and well above Power Planner's $4.99 lifetime and myHomework's ~$4.99/yr. Coursicle's $29.99 tier is close. Semli is mid-market.

## Momentum: growth and decline signals

### Takeaway
MyStudyLife is the largest dedicated planner and is pivoting to paid AI tiers, with some backlash from long-time users. Saturn was absorbed by Snap. School Planner is huge on Android. Shovel and Coursicle are small on iOS (hundreds of ratings). The legacy apps (iStudiez, myHomework, Egenda) appear stagnant. The many AI-first newcomers (DormWay, Feymark, Semora, StudyWillow) are crowding the category through SEO.

## Does the Canvas Student app cover "what's due" well enough?

### Takeaway
Partly. Canvas Student shows a cross-course To Do list, a calendar and a widget, and in 2025-2026 it added a coursework dashboard and AI study tools. It still covers only Canvas items that instructors give due dates, and it cannot hold work shifts, other LMSs or personal life. The April-May 2026 Canvas breach and outage also give a local-first planner a talking point.

### Cited Findings
- Canvas Student app provides course calendars, To Do items, notifications and Inbox — [Instructure Community](https://community.instructure.com/en/kb/articles/662784-what-is-the-canvas-app)
- The To Do list shows only upcoming course activities; personal to-dos added on mobile appear in the Calendar instead — [Instructure Community](https://community.instructure.com/en/kb/articles/662709-viewing-my-personal-to-do-items-in-the-canvas-student-app)
- The web To Do list shows up to 7 items; items stay for 4 weeks after the due date unless submitted — [school help desk](https://sas-lps.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/42000092946-canvas-to-do-list-calendar-canvas-notification-preferences-for-students), [Franklin](https://support.franklin.edu/hc/en-us/articles/360046317533-Canvas-To-Do-List-for-Students)
- The To-Do screen and widget were upgraded (Discussion Checkpoints); IgniteAI was replaced with "Study tools" in v8.10.x (~June 2026); v8.13.0 (Oct 12, 2026) fixed the To-do badge and favorites filter — [App Store (CA)](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/canvas-by-instructure/id480883488), [appstore.no mirror](https://appstore.no/apps/canvas-student.html)
- A new opt-in student dashboard with a "Centralized Coursework" widget launched May 25, 2026; instructors must add due dates for items to appear — [Rutgers](https://canvas.rutgers.edu/2026/05/25/canvas-student-dashboard-changes/)
- IgniteAI student study tools (Summarize, Quiz Me, Flashcards) are on only when the institution enables them — [U-M ITS](https://its.umich.edu/academics-research/teaching-learning/canvas/updates); some IgniteAI pieces were dropped after June 30, 2026 — [SMU](https://blog.smu.edu/itconnect/2026/06/26/igniteai-canvas-whats-next/)
- 2026 Canvas breach: intrusion around Apr 29 with a second incident May 7; ShinyHunters claimed it; names, emails, student IDs and private messages were exposed; scope claims up to ~275M users are unverified; platform fully operational by May 15 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Canvas_data_breach), [UMD IT](https://it.umd.edu/security-privacy-audit-risk-and-compliance-services-sparcs/topic-week/2026-canvas-data-breach-what-we-know), [BSK law firm](https://www.bsk.com/news-events-videos/deconstructing-the-canvas-instructure-data-breach-what-happened-and-what-should-your-institution-do-next); a reported deal with the hackers — [Security Affairs](https://securityaffairs.com/192059/cyber-crime/instructure-settles-with-hackers-following-massive-student-data-theft.html)

### Inferences
- For a student who takes only Canvas courses and has no job, Canvas Student plus its widget covers most of "what's due." Semli's value rests on what Canvas cannot hold: work shifts, overlaps, non-Canvas syllabi, multiple LMSs and urgent alarms.
- Semli's iCal feed import does not depend on Canvas API tokens. That matters after the breach, when schools may tighten third-party access (speculative).
